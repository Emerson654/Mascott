## What would flip a no

- A free-plan trial on my real site where the A/B report shows a clear lift in sign-ups within a couple of weeks, with the confirm-before-submit step working cleanly.
- A few real SaaS case studies showing a measured sign-up lift with zero bad actions, plus a read-only mode (answers and pointing only, no actions) that I could run on top of Intercom for a month and check in the A/B report first.
- Free-tier A/B data on my own site showing a clear sign-up lift, plus a handful of independent reviews on G2 or Capterra from edtech or kids' products, and a clear statement of how it handles minors' data.
- Seeing the A/B report show a clear lift on the free plan, plus the free tier hitting its limit so I'm forced to upgrade to Starter.
- A white-label Agency plan that I could resell to clients at a markup, with proof of a measured lift on a real site, so it's clearly less work than building and maintaining my own.
- A real A/B result from a comparable site showing a clear lift in sign-ups, after I've tested the free plan on my own traffic and the lift shows up in my numbers.
- A case study from a comparable consumer or edtech app showing a measured sign-up lift, plus the sober pro look working on my own site during the free trial.
- Seeing a real, sober-looking example on a site like mine, plus an A/B result from a comparable business showing a clear lift in sign-ups.
- Seeing a measured lift on the free plan with my own traffic, plus a case study from a site my size showing no wrong actions. Then I'd pay for Starter.
- Seeing the free tier's A/B report show a real lift on my own site, or a case study from a site of my size with numbers.
- A live demo on a real site like my clients' with a track record of no bad actions, plus a client actually asking me for it. Then I'd go to Agency.
- A real case study with a measured conversion lift on a site like my clients' (not a demo), plus being able to run the A/B on the free plan for a month before paying anything.

Buyers say they would buy **1.0 times** in the first month on average.
