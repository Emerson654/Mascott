# Board meeting: Mascott

**Vote: 2 FUND IF, 1 PASS. Average score 3.7 / 10.**
(Offers lens 4, FUND IF · Product lens 4, FUND IF · Monopoly lens 3, PASS. Memos in `founder/board/`.)

## Risks raised by more than one member (most dangerous first)
1. **No chosen market.** All three: "everyone with a website" is not a beachhead. Five or six segments with different pain, budgets and channels.
2. **The mascot is a skin, not the outcome.** Offers and Monopoly: nobody's pain is "my widget isn't cute". Sold as a prettier chatbot, it fights on price against Intercom, Tidio and Crisp, and incumbents could ship "custom avatar" as a toggle.
3. **The on-site agent is the weakest link.** Product and Offers: acting reliably on thousands of DOMs and checkouts is hard. A failure from a cute mascot looks like a joke at the merchant's expense. Setup effort kills the "no-code" promise.
4. **The distribution plan contradicts the kill rule.** Monopoly and Offers: SEO/GEO matures in 2-3 months, which is exactly when the 3-month rule fires. The first euro must come from somewhere else.
5. **Inference costs vs cheap flat plans.** Offers and Monopoly: heavy users can cost more than 19-29 EUR.

## Conditions (checklist for the rest of the pack)
- [ ] One segment and one outcome chosen before code (candidates: Shopify stores "find product / add to cart", or small SaaS "walk new users to activation").
- [ ] One platform and one action for v1. The general agent is dropped.
- [ ] The first five minutes are scripted and true: paste the snippet, and the mascot already knows the site.
- [ ] A designed failure state: the mascot fails gracefully, in character.
- [ ] 5-10 paying pilots or pre-paid commitments from direct outreach within 30 days. SEO/GEO is the slow channel, not the first euro.
- [ ] Cost per conversation measured; gross margin above ~70% at the entry price.
- [ ] Offer stack: done-for-you install, mascot within 24h, weekly conversion report, conditional guarantee.
- [ ] A defensible wedge: deep native actions on one platform plus a house style people ask for by name.

## The strongest version the board can see
Not what was pitched. A **Shopify (or one-platform) shopping-guide mascot**: installed from the app store in one click. It reads the catalogue on its own, then answers, **points at** and adds products to the cart, in a recognisable house style. It is sold on one number (assisted add-to-carts or checkouts), with a done-for-you setup and a refund if there is no lift in 30 days. The mascot is the brand and the bonus; the outcome is what is sold. The board disagrees on whether even this can last: the Monopoly lens expects copycats and incumbents within months, and passes unless 10 pre-paid commitments prove the niche first.
