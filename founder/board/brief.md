# Board brief: Mascott (working name)

Context: a French founder wants to test this SaaS idea before building it. Everything is hypothetical; nothing is built yet. The founder is the "president"; a CEO runs the work. The goal of this exercise is to decide: is it viable, who is the real target, what price, and how to get known.

- What it is: a SaaS that lets anyone who runs a website or a SaaS add an AI *agent* (not just a chatbot) embodied by a cute, fully customisable animated mascot. The mascot talks with visitors and can act on the site for them (navigate, fill forms, search the catalogue, start a checkout, book a slot, trigger in-app actions), installed through a JS snippet.
- Who it is for: people building or running a website or a SaaS who would otherwise add a chatbot widget (Intercom, Crisp, Tidio-style). The exact segment is unknown: indie hackers and small SaaS teams, e-commerce stores (Shopify, WooCommerce), web agencies and freelancers building client sites, SMB/showcase sites (Webflow, WordPress, Wix), edtech and consumer apps.
- What it sells, at what price: a monthly subscription. The mascot is generated from a prompt or a few checkboxes (species, colours, personality, outfit, brand palette) in a single house style: soft, "cocooning", adorable, similar in spirit to the friendly AI companion characters that big consumer AI apps have started shipping. All mascots share one predefined animation set (idle, wave, think, talk, point at an element on the page, celebrate, sleep), so generation is cheap and consistent. Starting price hypothesis (to be tested, not decided): Starter ~19-29 EUR/month, Pro ~79 EUR/month, usage-based above a quota of conversations/actions.
- Where and how: online, worldwide, English and French first. Acquisition planned mainly through SEO and GEO (getting cited by ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews), which typically takes 2-3 months to start producing traffic. Delivered as a no-code dashboard plus a script tag / npm package. Powered by third-party LLM APIs, so every conversation has a variable inference cost.
- Budget and constraints (ASSUMED by the CEO, the founder did not give them): solo founder or two people, bootstrapped, about 5,000 EUR of starting cash, no salary in year 1, the product is built with AI coding tools. Hard rule from the founder: if after 3 months on the market the business has made 0 EUR, it is considered dead and the strategy must change.
