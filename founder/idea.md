# Mascott (working name)

- What it is: a SaaS that lets anyone who runs a website or a SaaS add an AI *agent* (not just a chatbot) embodied by a cute, fully customisable animated mascot. The mascot talks with visitors and can act on the site for them (navigate, fill forms, search the catalogue, start a checkout, book a slot, trigger in-app actions), through a JS snippet.
- Who it is for: people building or running a website or a SaaS who would otherwise add a chatbot widget (Intercom/Crisp/Tidio-style). The exact segment is unknown and is what the simulation must find: indie hackers and small SaaS teams, e-commerce stores (Shopify, WooCommerce), web agencies and freelancers building client sites, SMB/showcase sites (Webflow, WordPress, Wix), edtech and consumer apps.
- What it sells, at what price: a monthly subscription. The mascot is generated from a prompt or a few checkboxes (species, colours, personality, outfit, brand palette) in one house style ("cocooning", soft, adorable, close to the friendly AI companions consumer apps ship today). All mascots share the same predefined animation set (idle, wave, think, talk, point at an element, celebrate, sleep), so generation is cheap. Starting hypothesis to test: Starter ~19-29 EUR/month, Pro ~79 EUR/month, usage-based above a quota of conversations/actions. **Price is a hypothesis, not decided.**
- Where and how: online, worldwide, English and French first. Acquisition planned mainly via SEO and GEO (being cited by ChatGPT/Perplexity/Google AI answers), which takes 2-3 months to start working. Delivered as a no-code dashboard + a script tag / npm package, LLM-powered (third-party LLM API, so every conversation has an inference cost).
- Budget and constraints (ASSUMED, not given by the founder - flag in every output): solo founder or 2 people, bootstrapped, about 5,000 EUR of starting cash, no salary in year 1, founder builds the product with AI coding tools. Hard rule set by the founder: if the business has still made 0 EUR after 3 months on the market, it is considered dead and the strategy must be changed.
