## What would flip a no

- Proof, with real numbers from a similar SaaS, that the mascot lifts onboarding or trial conversion over a plain chat widget, plus on-site actions that work on my app without custom engineering during the trial.
- Proof that the on-site actions are safe, meaning a hard allow-list of actions, a confirm step before anything that spends money or books something, and a full log I can review. It would also have to work alongside my current chat inbox so I don't have to migrate anything.
- Independent G2/Capterra reviews plus a case study showing a measurable lift in activation or conversion, and a clear answer on child data safety, with the free plan hitting its limit because it is actually being used.
- A free plan with several hundred conversations and no badge, or a one-time price under 10 EUR, with clear proof it lifts leads on a site like mine.
- A white-label or agency plan with a flat price across many client sites, unlimited conversations, and the mascot and animation set exportable so I can resell it as my own add-on.
- A real A/B test from a site like mine showing a clear lift in sign-ups or sales, plus on-site actions that work without me building them.
- A published A/B test or case study from a comparable SaaS showing a measurable lift in trial signups or activation, plus proof the script adds negligible load time (Lighthouse/Core Web Vitals numbers).
- A/B test data from a real site like mine showing the mascot raises conversions or bookings, plus a way to make the character look polished and non-childish.
- A serious, non-cartoon avatar style that fits a professional brand, plus a one-click import of my current chatbot's content and proof it converts better than what I have.
- A controlled A/B test or case study from a similar small SaaS showing a measurable lift in signups or conversion, plus hard guardrails that stop the agent from taking any action I haven't approved.
- A clear GDPR/DPA page with EU data hosting, plus a hard cap or predictable overage pricing, and a trial where it answers my own docs accurately.
- Hard data from comparable small sites that the mascot raises conversions, plus on-site actions on the Starter plan at around 15 EUR.
