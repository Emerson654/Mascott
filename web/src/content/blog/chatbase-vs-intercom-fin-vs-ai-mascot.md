## Chatbase: a docs chatbot, quickly

Chatbase lets you upload documents or point it at your website and embed a chatbot in minutes. Plans are credit-based: Hobby is reported at about $40 a month (or $32 on annual billing) with 500 credits and 5 AI actions; Standard at $120 and Pro at $400. Extra credits are about $40 per 1,000, and removing Chatbase branding is about $99 a month ([chatarmin](https://chatarmin.com/en/blog/chatbase-pricing)).

**Good:** fastest setup, low entry price, free tier for testing.

**Watch out for:** running out of credits, the branding fee, and answer quality. Some reviewers say it can "confidently generate totally wrong answers" ([Capterra](https://capterra.com/p/10012414/Chatbase/reviews/)), though most of those reviews date from 2023-24. One reviewer also reports being refused a small refund ([Capterra](https://www.capterra.com/p/10012414/Chatbase/reviews/?page=3)).

## Intercom Fin: support-grade, priced per resolution

Fin is Intercom's AI agent. It answers customers inside the Intercom messenger, hands off to human agents in the same inbox, and can follow procedures. It is billed at about **$0.99 per resolution**, on top of Intercom seats (Essential reported at $29 per seat per month on annual billing); several guides mention a 50-resolution monthly minimum ([Ringly](https://www.ringly.io/blog/intercom-pricing), [dev.to](https://dev.to/omar_bni_f6856a8bb0e021e9/intercom-fin-pricing-in-2026-the-real-math-behind-099-62j)).

**Good:** the strongest support workflow of the three, with human agents and AI in one inbox.

**Watch out for:** the bill grows with success. One review puts it bluntly: Fin "gets more expensive precisely as it gets better" ([sitegpt](https://sitegpt.ai/blog/intercom-fin-ai-review)). Reviewers also note it can struggle with nuance on complex issues ([hiverhq](https://hiverhq.com/blog/intercom-review)).

## How to keep any AI answer bot accurate

Wrong answers are the second most common complaint about this category, after pricing. The fixes are the same whichever tool you choose:

- **Clean the sources first.** Remove outdated help pages, duplicate articles and old pricing before connecting anything.
- **Write the ten answers that matter most.** Pricing, cancellation, data security, setup, integrations. Short, dated, unambiguous pages give short, correct answers.
- **Tell it what is out of scope.** Billing disputes, legal questions and account changes should go to a human.
- **Make it say "I don't know".** A bot that admits a gap and offers a person keeps trust; one that guesses loses it.
- **Read real conversations every week.** Twenty minutes of reading shows which docs to fix.
- **Re-test after every product change.** New features and renamed buttons are where answers go stale.

## FAQ

### Is Chatbase better than Intercom?

For a small site that only needs AI answers, Chatbase is simpler and cheaper. For a support team, Intercom is far more complete. They solve different problems.

### What is a good Intercom Fin alternative?

For support: Crisp or Tidio with their AI add-ons. For docs answers: Chatbase or CustomGPT.ai. For onboarding: an AI guide like Mascott. See [Best Intercom alternatives for small SaaS](/blog/best-intercom-alternatives-small-saas).

### Can an AI mascot work with Intercom?

Yes. The mascot guides and answers; when a user needs a person, it hands off to the Intercom messenger.

### Which is most accurate?

We have not run a public head-to-head accuracy test, so we will not claim one. All three answer from the content you provide, so the quality of your docs matters more than the vendor.
