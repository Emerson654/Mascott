**Short answer:** if you want live chat with AI for less hassle, the usual Tidio alternatives are **Crisp** (all-in-one chat for small teams), **Intercom** (premium support suite), **Chatbase** (AI docs chatbot) and, for Shopify stores, the free **Shopify Inbox**. If you run a SaaS and your real goal is getting new users to activation, an **AI onboarding guide** like Mascott fits better than any chat widget. If you run a small online store and want to start free, Tidio is still a sensible choice.

We make Mascott. Competitor prices are as reported by third-party guides in October 2026, with links.

## What Tidio is good at

Tidio bundles live chat, a ticket inbox, no-code chatbot flows and **Lyro**, an AI agent that answers from your knowledge base. It has a genuinely useful free plan (50 conversations a month and 50 lifetime Lyro conversations) and a Starter plan reported at **$29 per month for 100 conversations**. Growth plans start at about $59 and go up to $349 ([dragapp](https://www.dragapp.com/blog/tidio-pricing/), [eesel](https://eesel.ai/blog/tidio-pricing)).

It is popular with small businesses and Shopify stores, and it has around 1,100+ reviews on the Shopify App Store at 4.7 stars ([App Store](https://apps.shopify.com/reviews/2064651)).

## Why people look for an alternative

From public reviews and pricing analyses:

- **AI is an add-on with its own meter.** Lyro is reported from **$39 per month for 50 AI conversations**, then about **$0.70 per conversation** from 200 ([dragapp](https://www.dragapp.com/blog/tidio-pricing/)).
- **Every conversation counts the same.** A two-message chat and a twenty-message chat are each one conversation ([mooonai](https://blog.mooonai.com/en/tidio)).
- **Price surprises.** On G2, dozens of reviews are tagged "Expensive" or "Cost" ([getmacha](https://www.getmacha.com/blog/tidio-review)), and some users report automatic plan upgrades during traffic spikes and difficult refunds ([eesel](https://www.eesel.ai/blog/tidio-reviews)).
- **Scripted flows.** Lyro is reported to struggle with off-script questions inside Flows ([yourgpt](https://yourgpt.ai/blog/comparison/tidio-review)).

None of this makes Tidio a bad product. It means you should model your volume before choosing it.

## Crisp

Crisp is the most common like-for-like switch. Chat widget, shared inbox, knowledge base, and an AI assistant billed through monthly credits that do not roll over ([featurebase](https://www.featurebase.app/blog/crisp-pricing)). It fits small SaaS and service businesses that want one tool for chat and help docs.

**Choose it if** you want Tidio's scope with a different pricing shape.

## Intercom

Intercom is the premium option, with the strongest support features and Fin, its AI agent, billed at about $0.99 per resolution. For a team of two it is often more than needed. Our [Intercom alternatives guide](/blog/best-intercom-alternatives-small-saas) covers when it is worth it.

## Chatbase

If you mainly used Lyro to answer questions from your docs, Chatbase does that without the live-chat layer. Watch the credit limits and the reported ~$99 per month branding-removal add-on ([chatarmin](https://chatarmin.com/en/blog/chatbase-pricing)).

## Shopify Inbox

For Shopify stores, the free Shopify Inbox is hard to beat: it is free on every Shopify plan and includes an optional AI agent that uses your catalog and policies ([App Store](https://apps.shopify.com/chat)). Tidio's install count on Shopify is reported down 12.8% year on year, which suggests many stores are moving to the native option ([StoreLeads](https://storeleads.app/reports/shopify/app/tidio-chat)). If you sell on Shopify, try Inbox before paying for anything.

## Mascott

Mascott is not a live-chat tool. It is an AI guide with a face for websites and SaaS apps. It answers from your pages and docs and **points users to their next step** (for example, highlighting the "Connect your calendar" button). It runs on top of whatever chat you have, Tidio included, and hands off when someone wants a human.

**Choose it if** you run a SaaS and your issue is that users sign up and get lost. **Do not choose it if** you need a human chat inbox or you sell on Shopify: Tidio or Shopify Inbox fits better.

## Chat widget or onboarding guide?

These are two different jobs, and mixing them up is the most common reason teams end up unhappy with any tool.

| Question | Chat widget (Tidio, Crisp) | Onboarding guide (Mascott) |
|---|---|---|
| Main job | Answer incoming questions | Move new users to their first win |
| Starts the conversation | The visitor | The guide, at the right moment |
| Knows where the user is in the product | Rarely | Yes, it guides on the current page |
| Human handoff | Built in | Hands off to your chat tool |
| Typical buyer | Support or store owner | SaaS founder or product person |

If you are a SaaS founder, read [What is an AI onboarding agent?](/blog/what-is-an-ai-onboarding-agent) before choosing.

## What to check in any chat or AI tool before buying

Whichever tool you pick, the same few checks prevent most regrets:

- **How is AI usage counted?** Per conversation, per resolution, per message credit or flat. Ask for the definition in writing, then estimate a busy month, not an average one.
- **What happens at the limit?** Some tools stop answering, some upgrade you automatically, some bill overage. You want a warning first.
- **Where do answers come from?** The tool should use your own help pages and say when it does not know.
- **How does handoff work?** A visitor who wants a person should reach one in a click, with the conversation history attached.
- **Does it slow your site down?** Load the widget on a test page and check page speed before and after.
- **Can you test it on part of your traffic?** Showing it to half of new visitors is the only honest way to see if it helps.
- **Can you leave easily?** Export of conversations and knowledge sources, and clear cancellation and refund terms.

## How to switch away from Tidio

1. **Export** your Lyro knowledge sources and your most common questions.
2. **Count** last month's conversations and how many were handled by Lyro. Multiply by each alternative's reported pricing.
3. **Run in parallel** for a week where possible: new tool on some pages, Tidio on others.
4. **Cancel at the end of a billing period**, and check Tidio's refund terms first.

## FAQ

### What is the best free Tidio alternative?

Crisp has a free plan for two seats, and Shopify stores get Shopify Inbox free. Chatbase has a small free tier for AI answers.

### Is Crisp better than Tidio?

Crisp suits small teams that want chat, inbox and docs in one tool. Tidio suits stores that want to start free. Compare how each bills AI usage against your own volume.

### Does Mascott replace Tidio?

Only if you used Tidio purely for AI answers on a SaaS. If you need live chat with humans, keep Tidio (or Crisp) and add Mascott for onboarding.
